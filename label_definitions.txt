no_argument: A post that shares news, asks a genuine question, or discusses a topic without making an identifiable investment claim or market prediction.

unsupported: A post that makes an investment claim or market prediction without providing meaningful supporting evidence or reasoning.

partial: A post that makes an investment claim and provides relevant evidence or a causal explanation, but does not fully connect that support to its conclusion.

well_supported: A post that makes an investment claim supported by specific, checkable evidence and clear reasoning explaining how the evidence supports the conclusion.
